# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Shuqi Liu, Yuzhou Cao, Lei Feng, Bo An, and Luke Ong for the
paper, its formal framing of multi-expert learning to defer, and the
experimental details that make careful independent scrutiny possible.

This repository is intended as a respectful audit and reproduction record.
The positive mathematical checks are credited at their actual scope, the
Table 2 and Theorem 6(A) discrepancies are reported narrowly, and the
unfinished MiceBone campaign remains marked as unfinished. No result here
should be read as an accusation about the authors or as a substitute for
author clarification.
